# puipui app icon concepts

C / resting loaf is the selected icon. The other candidates are retained for reference.

## Guinea pig direction

- `guinea-pig-a.svg`: round body with short feet, side view.
- `guinea-pig-b.svg`: front-facing cheeks and small ears.
- `guinea-pig-c.svg`: resting body with a closed eye.

All three are original vector sketches. The Figma board includes light/dark app tiles and 16/18/24px monochrome previews.

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/spwA1eEgycGIK1TtjHZ45R/puipui?node-id=27-134

## Earlier abstract directions

- `a.svg`: paired lowercase p, original geometry.
- `b.svg`: two smooth pulses, original geometry.
- `c.svg`: Lucide terminal geometry, under the license in `../../Sources/Puipui/Resources/Icons/LICENSE`.

The editable symbol masters use a 24 × 24 view box, 2-unit centered strokes, round caps and joins, and no fill. Figma shows separate rounded application backgrounds and small monochrome menu-bar previews. The background is not part of the Lucide symbol specification.

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/spwA1eEgycGIK1TtjHZ45R/puipui?node-id=25-134
